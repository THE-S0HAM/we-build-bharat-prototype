# Speaker Reimbursement Policy

## Travel

- Economy class flights reimbursed for speakers traveling from outside the city
- Train tickets (AC 2-Tier or above) reimbursed for domestic travel
- Local taxi expenses reimbursed with receipts (max INR 3,000 per day)
- Speakers must book travel themselves and submit receipts after the event

## Accommodation

- Up to 2 nights hotel accommodation provided for outstation speakers
- Hotel arranged by the organizing team (not self-booked)
- Additional nights at speaker's expense unless pre-approved

## Per Diem

- No per diem for local speakers
- INR 1,500 per day for outstation speakers (covers meals not provided at venue)

## Reimbursement Process

1. Speaker submits receipts within 14 days after the event
2. CommunityOps reviews receipts against policy
3. If within policy: auto-processed
4. If exceeds policy: flagged for organizer approval
5. Reimbursement via bank transfer within 21 business days

## Commitment Rules

- Accommodation booking is a HIGH_RISK action (financial commitment)
- SpeakerOps agent must request approval before booking
- Travel arrangements are the speaker's responsibility — agent only tracks status
